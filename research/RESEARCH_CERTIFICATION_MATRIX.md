## Certification Levels

- **C0 Draft**: idea captured, not yet connected to implementation.
- **C1 Review Packet**: paper exists with explicit role and proof cautions.
- **C2 Contracted**: schema, manifest, or registry entry exists.
- **C3 Implemented**: runnable code or adapter exists.
- **C4 Verified**: tests, receipts, benchmarks, or release artifacts prove the claim.
- **C5 Market-Ready**: docs, SDK, UX, support path, and proof signal are present.

## Truth Line

Research is valuable because it carries architecture, not because it overclaims implementation. This repo should preserve both: ambitious doctrine and honest proof status.
